DEDUPLICATION AND SPLIT VERIFICATION
========================================

1. Source: data/labels/train.csv, val.csv, test.csv (original stratified 70/15/15 split of
   dr_unified_v2, random_state=42). Originals are kept unchanged.

2. Exact duplicate detection: MD5 of file bytes, after grouping by byte size.
   - Duplicate groups (distinct content): 188
   - Files inside those groups: 383
   - Extra copies: 195

3. Severity label conflicts: 15 groups (14 pairs, 1 triplet), 31 files.
   Policy: every member of a conflict group is excluded from all splits.

4. Train/test leakage before dedup: 39 groups had members in both train and test.

5. Dedup rule for the remaining groups:
   - Priority train > val > test.
   - Within a split, keep the first copy in CSV order.
   - Remove all other copies.

6. Removed rows: 210 total (179 uniform-group copies + 31 conflict-group files).
   Per split: train 116 removed, val 46 removed, test 48 removed.

7. Resulting split sizes:
   - train_dedup.csv: 64,634
   - val_dedup.csv: 13,829
   - test_dedup.csv: 13,828
   - Total: 92,291 (92,291 + 210 = 92,501)

8. Verification results (all passed):
   - Row counts match the numbers above.
   - Columns match the originals.
   - All image paths exist on disk (0 missing).
   - None of the 31 conflict files appear in any dedup file.
   - No MD5 value appears in more than one dedup row.
   - No MD5 value is shared between train_dedup and test_dedup.

9. Limitation: MD5 detects only byte-identical files. Re-encoded, resized, or cropped
   copies of the same fundus image are not detected. Near-duplicate detection
   (for example perceptual hashing) is not yet done.

10. Original split files (train.csv, val.csv, test.csv) are retained. Training in
    Week 2 uses the *_dedup.csv files.
